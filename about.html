Our story

About The BookNest

A century-old institution, still turning pages.

Founded on one idea

In 1962, a single donated shelf of books grew into a reading room, then a wing, then a building of its own.

BookNest was built on the idea that access to knowledge shouldn't depend on what you can afford — only on your willingness to ask.

Today the library holds close to twenty thousand titles across eight collections, staffed by librarians who still hand-stamp every due date. We've kept the card catalog on display, not out of nostalgia, but because it reminds every visitor that a library is, at its core, an index of human curiosity.

Open Access

Every card is free. No late fees on children's titles, ever.

Quiet Craft

Reading rooms are kept for reading — phones on silent, always.

Living Archive

We restore, digitize, and preserve as much as we acquire.
